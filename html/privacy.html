Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FarmRent collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

FarmRent is committed to respecting the privacy of people who use our platform. We collect and process information only for legitimate purposes connected with providing, securing, improving, and supporting our services.

By using FarmRent, you acknowledge that your information may be processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws in Nigeria.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use FarmRent.

  • Name and account information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Information submitted when creating an account.
  • Equipment listing information.
  • Information connected to bookings and rental activities.
  • Information you provide when contacting our support team.
  • Basic technical and usage information required to operate and secure the website.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is being processed.

3. How We Use Your Information

FarmRent may use information collected through the platform to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide equipment rental services.
  • Allow users to list equipment.
  • Support agro-product activities.
  • Process and manage bookings.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Send important service-related communications.
  • Improve the website and user experience.
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent, abusive, or unauthorized activity.
  • Protect the security and integrity of FarmRent.
  • Comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

4. Account Information

When you create a FarmRent account, information such as your name, email address, telephone number, and login credentials may be collected.

Passwords should never be stored or handled in plain text by FarmRent's backend systems. Where authentication is implemented, passwords should be securely hashed using appropriate industry-standard security practices.

You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and should notify FarmRent if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

5. Equipment Listing Information

If you list equipment on FarmRent, the information you submit may be displayed to other users where necessary to operate the marketplace.

This may include equipment details, photographs, rental information, location-related information, and other information that you choose to include in your listing.

Do not include passwords, bank passwords, payment card security codes, national identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in a public equipment listing.

6. Booking Information

When you book equipment through FarmRent, information associated with the booking may be processed to manage the rental, communicate with relevant parties, maintain booking records, and provide customer support.

Only information reasonably required to complete and manage a booking should be requested.

7. Payment Information

Where FarmRent uses a third-party payment provider, payment information may be processed directly by that provider.

FarmRent does not intend to store complete payment card numbers, card PINs, or card security codes on its own servers.

Payment providers may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review those policies when completing a payment.

8. When We Share Information

FarmRent does not sell or rent users' personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide a service requested by you.
  • Process a booking or transaction.
  • Provide hosting, security, communication, analytics, or other technical services.
  • Respond to lawful requests from government or regulatory authorities.
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, security incidents, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Protect the rights, property, safety, and security of FarmRent, its users, or others.

Service providers that process information on our behalf should only receive information reasonably necessary for the service they provide.

9. Data Security

FarmRent takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, misuse, or destruction.

Depending on the system involved, security measures may include access controls, secure authentication, password hashing, encrypted connections, restricted administrative access, monitoring, backups, and other appropriate safeguards.

Access to personal information should be limited to people or service providers who need the information for legitimate FarmRent operations.

However, no internet service can guarantee that information will be completely immune from every possible security incident. FarmRent cannot guarantee that unauthorized persons will never attempt or succeed in accessing information.

If FarmRent becomes aware of a personal-data security incident that requires notification under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to investigate, contain, and respond to the incident and make any required notifications.

10. Bots, Scraping, and Automated Access

FarmRent does not authorize bots, crawlers, scrapers, automated scripts, or other automated systems to collect personal information from the platform except where access is expressly permitted for legitimate technical purposes.

Automated attempts to access restricted areas, collect user information, bypass security controls, interfere with the platform, or misuse FarmRent services are prohibited.

FarmRent may use reasonable security controls such as authentication, rate limiting, access restrictions, monitoring, and other measures to help detect and prevent unauthorized automated access.

These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee that every malicious bot or automated attack will be prevented.

11. How Long We Keep Information

FarmRent will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, preventing fraud, and complying with legal obligations.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally appropriate.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Ask questions about how your information is being processed.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority where applicable.

Some requests may be subject to verification and legal limitations.

13. Cookies and Similar Technologies

FarmRent may use cookies or similar technologies that help the website operate, remember preferences, maintain sessions, improve security, and understand how the website is used.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

14. Children's Privacy

FarmRent is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to FarmRent inappropriately, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

15. Third-Party Services

FarmRent may rely on third-party providers for services such as hosting, payment processing, authentication, communications, analytics, security, and other technical functions.

Those providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. FarmRent will seek to use providers that apply appropriate security and privacy practices.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

FarmRent may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal requirements, or data practices change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective or update date. Continued use of the platform after an update may be subject to the updated policy where permitted by law.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise a privacy right, or believe your information has been handled improperly, please contact FarmRent through the contact information provided on our website.

Need help with a privacy concern?

Contact our support team and provide enough information for us to understand and respond to your request.
