ABOUT FARМRENT

Connecting farmers with the resources they need.

FarmRent is an agricultural platform designed to make it easier for farmers to find farm equipment and agricultural products while helping owners and sellers reach the people who need them.

WHO WE ARE

Making agricultural resources easier to access.

FarmRent is a platform created to connect farmers with agricultural resources in a simpler and more convenient way. Instead of searching through different places to find equipment or farm supplies, farmers can use FarmRent to discover available options in one place.

The platform also gives equipment owners and agro product sellers an opportunity to showcase what they have available and connect with farmers looking for those resources.
